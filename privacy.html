Ibex Climb

Privacy policy · Last updated 6 November 2026

In short: Ibex Climb has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, phone number or contacts. Your progress stays on your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob and measures usage through Google Analytics for Firebase, which collect device identifiers and usage data as described below. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we are

Ibex Climb is a mobile game published by SanSid Media, Bengaluru, India. In this policy, "we" means SanSid Media.

What the game stores on your device

To remember where you are, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • the stars you earned on each level;
  • your best altitude in the endless climb;
  • your in-game coin balance and what you have unlocked.

This data is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in your device settings.

Information collected by third-party services

The app connects to the internet to show ads, measure usage and process purchases. These services are operated by Google LLC and its affiliates under their own privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob (advertising). Advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data. Used to show ads, measure ad performance, limit how often ads appear, prevent fraud and, where you have consented, personalise ads.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (usage measurement). App instance ID, Advertising ID where available, device model, operating system version, language, approximate location from IP address and in-app events (for example levels started, cleared or failed, ads viewed and in-app purchases). Used to understand how the game is played so we can balance difficulty and improve it.
  • Google Play Billing (purchases). Purchase records (product, time and a purchase token). Payment details stay with Google and are never seen by us.

The web playtest page of the game is served by GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. As with any web page, those providers may receive your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store this information.

Consent and your choices

  • Ad consent. Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message before ads are personalised.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings, then Privacy, then Ads, on most devices). This limits ad personalisation.
  • Rewarded ads are optional. Ads that let you continue a climb or double your coins play only when you choose them.
  • Remove Ads. A one-time purchase turns off the ads that appear between levels.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all information stored on your device.

Purchases

In-app purchases (coin packs, the Starter pack, Remove Ads and the rescue pack) are processed by Google Play under Google's terms. The game contains no random or loot-box purchases, and every item shows what it contains before you buy. Items bought through Google Play can be restored from your Google account.

Data retention and security

Information on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Analytics event data is kept by Google Analytics for Firebase for the retention period we set, which is no longer than 14 months. Advertising data is retained by Google under its own policies. Information sent by the app to Google's services is encrypted in transit.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and US state privacy laws, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, to withdraw consent and to raise a complaint with a regulator. Because the app has no accounts, we hold no information that identifies you by name. To make a request, contact us using the details below; we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and is listed for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and information about them has been collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
