Ibex Climb

Privacy policy · Last updated 5 November 2026

In short: Ibex Climb has no accounts, collects no personal information and sends no data to us or to anyone else. Your progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Ibex Climb is a mobile game published by SanSid Media, Bengaluru, India. In this policy, "we" means SanSid Media.

What the game stores on your device

To remember where you are, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • the stars you earned on each level;
  • your best altitude in the endless climb;
  • your in-game coin balance and what you have unlocked.

This data is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in your device settings.

Internet use

The Android app works without an internet connection and does not send any data.

The web playtest page of the game is served by GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. As with any web page, those providers may receive your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store this information.

Ads, analytics and purchases

The game does not currently show third-party advertising, does not use advertising identifiers and does not include analytics or tracking tools.

If a future version adds ads or in-app purchases, we will update this policy before that version is released, and describe the providers involved, the data they handle and the choices available to you. Purchases made through Google Play are processed by Google; we do not receive your payment details.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
